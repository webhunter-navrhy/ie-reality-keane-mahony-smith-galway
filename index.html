Keane Mahony Smith are pleased to present this 0.5 acre site at Castleblakeney village being sold subject to planning permission.

Castleblakeney is a village 7 km East of Menlough. The site is located approximately 500 metres from the village of Castleblakeney on the R359 and in close proximity to the National School. The location offers easy access to Ballinasloe and Galway City.

The village has National & Secondary schools, shop, pubs and a GAA pitch. All essential amenities can be found in Castleblakeney and surrounding towns.

There is no enurement clause attached to this site.
